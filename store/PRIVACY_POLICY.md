# Privacy Policy for Papertrail PDF

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Papertrail PDF is designed to work privately and offline. The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

## Documents and app data

PDF documents selected by the user are copied into the app's private on-device storage so they remain available in the library. Reading position, recent-document information, and appearance preferences are also stored only on the device. This information is not sent to Papertrail PDF or any third party.

Users can remove an imported document from the library at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data according to the operating system's normal behavior.

## Permissions

Papertrail PDF uses the operating system's document picker only when the user chooses to open a file. It does not request unrestricted access to photos or device storage.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking technology.

## Children's privacy

Because Papertrail PDF collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the related feature is released.

## Contact

Questions may be sent to `support@papertrailpdf.app`.

This policy must be published at `https://papertrailpdf.app/privacy` (or another public URL) before app-store submission.
