## Full description

Papertrail PDF is a calm, private reader built for focused reading.

Open PDFs from your device, zoom in on the details, follow document links, select text, and return exactly where you stopped. Your recent documents are organized into a simple on-device library, ready whenever you need them.

Highlights:

- Smooth PDF reading and pinch-to-zoom
- Quick page navigation
- Automatic reading-position restore
- Light and dark appearance
- Private on-device document library
- Works completely offline
- No account, ads, analytics, or tracking

Your documents remain on your device. Papertrail PDF does not upload or sell your data.
